# Grace and Gladness: design rules

These rules apply to every page on graceandgladness.com, new or existing.
Follow them unless Geb asks for something else. When a rule and a request
conflict, do what Geb asked and mention which rule it breaks.

The goal: the site should look made by a careful person, not generated by a
template. Calm, plain, old, handmade. Think of a well-bound book, not a
startup landing page.

## 1. Declutter

- One page, one job. Each screen has one main message and at most one main button.
- Never offer the same links twice on one screen (for example a top menu and a row of cards that repeat it).
- No badges, pills, "est." tags, ratings, emoji, or decorative icons.
- No cards inside cards. Use a card only when it is a link the visitor can click.
- If an element does not help the visitor understand or act, remove it.

## 2. Type

- One typeface family: Cormorant Garamond. No second display font.
- No two-colour headings and no italic accent word inside a heading (the "Discover the Untamed *Wilderness*" pattern). Headings are one colour and one weight.
- The wordmark "Grace and Gladness" is plain text in one colour (ink). Never split it into coloured or italic parts.
- Colour belongs to small things only: links, the button, a verse reference, a short rule.
- Use `text-wrap: balance` on headings and short lines, so no line ends on a lone word.

## 3. Size and alignment

- Headings are smaller than you think. Home page name: about 46 to 80px. Page titles: about 44 to 64px.
- Left-align text. Centre only a single short line when there is a clear reason.
- Body text lines stay between about 45 and 75 characters wide (`max-width` around 32 to 36em).
- Let pictures carry the page. Text sits in the calm, empty part of a picture (usually the sky), never on top of busy detail.

## 4. Spacing

- Generous, even space. When in doubt, add space and remove an element.
- Group related things tightly and separate groups widely. Spacing should show what belongs together.
- Keep a wide outer margin: at least 20px on phones and about 5vw on wide screens.

## 5. Words

- Plain words and short sentences. No filler, no hype, no em dashes.
- Say one specific thing. Avoid brochure phrases such as "discover," "unlock," "journey," "transform," "world-class," "unforgettable."
- Headings state what the page is. Buttons say exactly where they go ("Start with the Bible in Three Pictures"), not "Learn more" or "Get started."
- The site's line is: "Plain teaching for people who want to be glad in the grace of God." Do not invent new taglines.
- Never invent facts, quotes, statistics or stories. Bible quotes must be checked against a real translation and cite the reference.

## 6. Colour (Warm Earth)

- Paper `#FAF8EC`, panel `#F3E9D2`, ink `#3A2A1C`, soft ink `#6A5741`, olive `#4E5B2A`, dark olive `#353F1B`, rust `#9A4A1E`, line `#D9C9A6`.
- Use the CSS variables in `assets/site.css` (`--paper`, `--ink`, `--grace`, and so on), never new hex values.
- Every text colour must pass WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1 for body text). Check it; do not assume.

## 7. Imagery

- Use the watercolour painting and art made in the same hand-painted style. No stock photos, no 3D renders, no gradients standing in for art, no AI sparkle or glow effects.
- One picture per screen is enough. Let it bleed to the edges; do not frame it in a rounded card.
- Never depict Christ.
- Pictures that are only decoration get `alt=""` and `aria-hidden="true"`.

## 8. Motion

- Motion is slow, quiet and plays once. Nothing loops. Nothing bounces.
- Allowed: the opening reveal, words fading in once, an arrow sliding a few pixels on hover, a menu preview fading in.
- Every animation stops under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.
- Never make visitors wait more than about 2 seconds to read the page.

## 9. Small crafted details (use these consistently)

- Buttons with an arrow: the arrow moves about 5px right on hover.
- Main menu items may show a small picture of where they lead on hover (mouse devices only, via `@media (hover: hover)`).
- Links are underlined or clearly coloured. Focus outlines stay visible.

## 10. Before you finish any page

- Check it at desktop (1440 wide) and phone (390 wide). Nothing scrolls sideways.
- Check contrast and keyboard focus.
- Remove anything left over from an earlier version.
- Read the page against rules 1 to 5 and cut whatever breaks them.

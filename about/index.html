Why Grace and Gladness

“When he came and saw the grace of God, he was glad, and he exhorted them all to remain faithful to the Lord with steadfast purpose.”

Acts 11:23

Barnabas came to Antioch and found people who had just believed. He saw the grace of God in them and was glad. Then he went and found Saul, and the two of them stayed a whole year and taught them.

This site exists for the same reason: to help hungry believers grow. It is for anyone who wants to understand the Bible and has not yet been shown how.

Scripture Memory

Walk through a book of the Bible.

Each book is a small ancient town you can walk through. Every room holds five verses, each tied to one object. Walk the same path enough times and the words come back when you picture the place. I call it location-triggered memory.

How to use it

Learn the verses by rote first. Then walk the town and place each verse on its object as you go. Make your own pictures; they stick better than anyone else's.

It works best on a laptop or tablet. Use the arrow keys or the on-screen joystick to walk, and drag to look around.

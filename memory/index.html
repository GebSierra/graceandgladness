Why Grace and Gladness.

Memorize a book of the Bible with a walkable location.

When Augustine wrote his classic work, The Confessions, he described walking through his own memory palace: “the fields and spacious palaces of my memory.”

An LTM, a location-triggered memory, is more often called a memory palace. It is an ancient technique that uses our spatial memory. The locations below are built to help you memorize books of the Bible.

Each book is a small ancient town you can walk through, with a location for each verse. If you have ever wanted to memorize books of the Bible verse by verse, this is one of the most powerful ways to do it.

How to use it

Learn the verses by rote first. Then walk the town and place each verse on its object as you go. Make your own pictures; they stick better than anyone else's.

It works best on a laptop or tablet. Use the arrow keys or the on-screen joystick to walk, and drag to look around.
